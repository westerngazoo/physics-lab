Why the motion is exact. The dissector is the derivation's conscience. Every textbook says "for small angles, sin θ ≈ tan θ" and moves on. Here the substitution is a measured quantity with a live budget: at amplitude ×1 the error is a fraction of a percent; at ×2.4 it is plainly visible in the split arrows. The wave equation is exactly as trustworthy as that number — knowing where a model's one approximation lives is worth more than memorizing its conclusion.
